PLATFORMS

The right robot for the right environment.

DELV is platform-agnostic. We help clients select against requirements, not vendor preference.

HUMANOID

Built for human environments

Operates in spaces designed for people. Suited to reception, security patrol, inspection and customer-facing roles in government and enterprise facilities.

QUADRUPED

Built for complex terrain

Four-legged platforms for inspection and environments where wheeled robots cannot go. Ideal for infrastructure monitoring, site inspection and remote assessment.

WHEELED

Built for structured environments

Reliable, cost-effective platforms for indoor operations. Suited to logistics, delivery, patrol, asset tracking and repetitive movement tasks.
